RIFTBOUND — TEMPORARY KEYWORD INTERACTIONS (REFERENCE)
=======================================================
Scope: How the TEMPORARY keyword interacts with reaction timing, HIDDEN cards
(esp. Sprite Call), spell speeds, and Dusk Rose Lab.
Authority note: Card-interaction rulings below are drawn from the RiftJudge
community rules database (rules-cited). Treat RiftJudge / a floor judge as the
final word on edge cases. Rules can shift with set patches (current set: Vendetta,
released 2026-07-31).


1. TEMPORARY — WHAT IT IS
-------------------------
- Card text pattern: "Kill it at the start of its controller's Beginning Phase,
  before scoring."
- It is a TRIGGERED ABILITY (a death trigger), NOT a silent auto-removal. At the
  start of the Beginning Phase it is placed on the chain. Because it uses the
  chain, it can be RESPONDED TO before it resolves.
- The check happens ONCE, only at the start of the turn — NOT when a unit enters.
  Consequence: a Temporary unit CREATED mid-turn (after the check) survives the
  current turn and is only checked at the controller's NEXT Beginning Phase.
- Multiple instances of Temporary are redundant (do not stack).


2. BEGINNING PHASE STRUCTURE (why "before scoring" matters)
-----------------------------------------------------------
The Beginning Phase resolves in order:
  (a) Beginning Step — "at the start of Beginning Phase" effects happen here,
      including Temporary kills and things like Dusk Rose Lab.
  (b) Scoring Step — Hold points are scored here.
Temporary kills occur in (a), BEFORE (b). So if you can keep/replace a body
through the Beginning Step, you still score in the Scoring Step.

- A Temporary unit CAN hold a battlefield and score. The keyword does not restrict
  holding or scoring. If it is present at the Scoring Step, it scores normally.


3. CORE LINE — SAVING VALUE WITH A HIDDEN CARD (e.g., Sprite Call)
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Goal: your only unit at a battlefield is a Temporary token about to die; you want
to keep the battlefield (hold + score) instead of losing it.

Why it works: the Temporary death trigger opens a reaction window. While that
trigger is still PENDING on the chain, the old token is still alive, so you STILL
control the battlefield — which means a card HIDDEN at that battlefield is still
valid and usable.

Sequence:
  1. Beginning Phase starts. Temporary trigger goes on the chain (not resolved yet).
  2. IN RESPONSE (old token still alive → you still control the BF → hidden card
     still valid): flip your Hidden card. Sprite Call from Hidden = Reaction speed,
     cost 0, and MUST be played to the SAME battlefield it was hidden at.
  3. Reaction resolves first → a NEW Sprite token enters that battlefield.
  4. The original Temporary trigger now resolves → kills the OLD token. You keep
     control via the new token.
  5. The new token survives (its Temporary check window already passed).
  6. Scoring Step → new token holds → you score.

CRITICAL RULE OF THUMB:
  RESPOND to the trigger; do NOT let it resolve first. If you let the old token
  die before you have a replacement, you drop to zero units at that battlefield,
  LOSE CONTROL, and LOSE the face-down Hidden card before you can use it.


4. THE GATE IS SPEED, NOT COST
------------------------------
Three spell timings in Riftbound:
  - NEUTRAL (no keyword): only on your turn, in Neutral (not in a showdown), and
    only when NO chain is open. (Sorcery-speed.)
  - ACTION: on your turn or in showdowns, but only in an OPEN state (no pending
    chain / when you hold focus). CANNOT respond to a chain item.
  - REACTION: the ONLY speed that can be added in response to something already on
    the chain.

Applied to the Temporary trigger (a chain item):
  - Only REACTION-speed cards/abilities may respond to it.
  - You are NOT limited to free Hidden cards. Any Reaction-speed card works, and
    paid ones are fine: you may EXHAUST (tap) runes for Energy and recycle for
    Power to play a Reaction spell from hand, or flash in an AMBUSH unit (Ambush =
    Reaction speed) — note Ambush needs "a battlefield where you have units," which
    is satisfied while the old token is still alive.
  - You may NOT play a plain ACTION or NEUTRAL spell in response, no matter how
    much Energy you have Ready. It is a timing/speed wall, not a cost wall.


5. HIDDEN OVERRIDES A CARD'S PRINTED SPEED
------------------------------------------
- Sprite Call is printed as an ACTION spell ("Play on your turn or in showdowns").
  It also has HIDDEN ("Hide now for [A] to react with later for [0]").
- These describe two DIFFERENT ways to play the same card:
    * From HAND  → uses ACTION timing → CANNOT respond to the Temporary trigger.
    * From HIDDEN → Hidden grants REACTION speed for that play, for 0 → CAN respond.
- Therefore the line requires PRE-COMMITMENT: you must have Hidden the card on an
  EARLIER turn (paying the [A] Hide cost then). A Sprite Call sitting in hand when
  the trigger fires is useless for this — from hand it is Action speed.
- This generalizes to ANY card with Hidden: Hidden is the mechanism for playing a
  normally-Action effect at Reaction speed in windows its printed timing would lock
  it out of.

HIDDEN CARD RESTRICTIONS (relevant context):
  - A Hidden card sits face-down at a SPECIFIC battlefield you control.
  - If you lose control of that battlefield while the card is still hidden, you
    LOSE the card.
  - Limit 1 Hidden card per battlefield.
  - When played from Hidden, it must target/affect the SAME battlefield where it
    was hidden (cannot reach another battlefield).


6. DUSK ROSE LAB COMBO
----------------------
Dusk Rose Lab (Battlefield, UNL-209):
  "At the start of your Beginning Phase, you may kill a unit you control here to
   draw 1. (This happens before scoring.)"

Base interaction (confirmed, RiftJudge FAQ #9231 / ruling #9428):
  - Both the Dusk Rose Lab trigger and the Temporary trigger go on the chain at the
    start of your Beginning Phase.
  - You control both → you choose the order they resolve.
  - Resolve Dusk Rose Lab FIRST: kill your doomed Temporary token as the ability's
    cost, draw 1. Then the Temporary trigger tries to resolve, finds its unit
    already gone, and is IGNORED (fizzles).
  - Net: you convert a token that was dying anyway into a drawn card.

Full combo (Dusk Rose Lab + Temporary + Hidden Sprite Call):
  Goal: draw a card off the dying token AND keep a token to hold/score.
  Because the Lab consumes the old token, the Hidden flip supplies the replacement.

  1. Beginning Phase starts. Temporary trigger + Dusk Rose Lab trigger both on the
     chain. You have priority to respond.
  2. Flip Hidden Sprite Call FIRST, while the OLD token is still alive (so you
     never lose control / never lose the Hidden card). New Sprite enters the same
     battlefield. You now have two tokens there.
  3. Resolve Dusk Rose Lab: kill the OLD token as the cost → draw 1.
  4. Resolve the Temporary trigger: old token already dead → ignored. (New token's
     own Temporary is not checked until next turn.)
  5. Scoring Step: new token holds → score.

  Result: +1 card (off a doomed body), battlefield kept, point scored.

  DO NOT: resolve Dusk Rose Lab and kill your only token BEFORE flipping the Hidden
  card. That drops you to zero units → lose control → lose the Hidden card first.
  Always flip the Hidden card while the old token is still alive.

This is an "aristocrats"-style engine: a disposable/doomed body becomes card
advantage + a replacement body + a held point, every Beginning Phase.


7. QUICK CHECKLIST (decision order at start of Beginning Phase)
--------------------------------------------------------------
  [ ] Is a Temporary token about to die and is it my only unit at that BF?
  [ ] Do I have a Hidden card at THAT battlefield? (Reaction speed when flipped.)
  [ ] Is the responder REACTION speed? (Hidden / Reaction spell / Ambush = yes.
      Plain Action/Neutral from hand = no, even with Energy.)
  [ ] Respond to the trigger — do NOT let it resolve first.
  [ ] If Dusk Rose Lab present: flip the Hidden replacement FIRST, THEN feed the
      old token to the Lab, THEN let Temporary fizzle.
  [ ] Confirm the replacement is present at the Scoring Step to hold/score.
