# Patterns & verified rulings — Lillia Fae Fawn

Everything here was checked against the Core Rules, the official FAQ, or the
printed card. Rule numbers are from the repo's **2025-06-02** PDF unless marked
otherwise — that printing is a revision behind current numbering (see the
`riftbound-rules-sources` memory).

## The scoring model — the thing the whole deck hangs on

- **Conquer scores immediately** (630.1). Gaining control of a battlefield you
  haven't scored this turn earns a point right then.
- **Temporary blocks Hold, not Conquer.** Tokens die at the start of your
  Beginning Phase (515.2.a), *before* the Scoring Step (515.2.b), and control
  requires a body present (181.4.d). So a Sprite alone can never Hold — but it
  scores fine by walking onto uncontested ground.
- **The Final Point rule — 632.1.b.** At 7 points, a Conquer only wins if you
  scored **every** battlefield that turn; otherwise you just draw a card. Only a
  **Hold** wins unconditionally. This is why the deck needs non-Temporary bodies
  and why "no bombs" was really "no way to take the last point."
- **The re-conquer loop.** Because your Sprites die each Beginning Phase, you
  *lose your own battlefield every turn*, which makes it conquerable again — one
  point per turn without ever holding.

## Card rulings that took work

- **Fae Fawn's Sprite is left at the location she moved FROM**, and it enters
  **exhausted** — her printed text lacks "ready", unlike the Legend ability,
  Sprite Call, Sprite Burst and Trevor. Verified against the card image. An
  exhausted Sprite at base can never act (standard move costs Exhaust, 140.2)
  and dies before your next turn. So: land her *on a battlefield you control*
  (559.2) and move her out later; moving her out of base wastes the trigger.
- **Zilean's copies enter ready.** Official FAQ: *"Both tokens enter ready when
  they resolve this way, because the copy generated by Zilean's replacement
  effect inherits any modifications (rule 375)."* Temporary is inherited by the
  same rule.
- **Recall is not a Move (617)**, so Fae Fawn recalled after a failed attack
  gives you no Sprite.
- **Only Sprite Call is castable from Hidden.** 723.1.d requires every choice to
  be at the hidden battlefield; Smoke and Mirrors and Resonating Strike both
  need a target at a *different* location, so they have no legal target set.
- **Gear can only be played to base (144.2)**, so Sprite Fountain never
  interacts with LeBlanc and always delivers its Deathknell Sprite.
- **Protector of Dreams' Tank grant has no location clause** — contrast
  LeBlanc's "at my battlefield". Protector works from base, where she can't be
  attacked (106.2.d).
- **LeBlanc is a mode switch, not an engine.** Stopping Sprites dying trades
  your Conquer point for a Hold point at the same rate of one per turn. Her real
  value is the *Final Point rule* and protecting real units — not extra points.

## Combat and priority

- **The Chain resolves one item at a time**, last-in-first-out, with a full
  priority round between each resolution (543.4). Not all at once.
- **Closed State = only [Reaction].** [Action] is not enough to respond to a
  spell. In this deck: Reactions are Stupefy, Smoke Screen, Discipline,
  Resonating Strike, Lilting Lullaby. Actions are Sprite Call, Smoke and
  Mirrors, Crescent Strike — they can never answer anything.
- **Permanents can't be responded to** (538) — the unit lands; only its play
  trigger can be answered.
- **[Ambush] units can be played as a Reaction** to a battlefield where you have
  units. That's the exception to "no units during a showdown."
- **No window between the showdown closing and damage** (626.1). Commit every
  pump and debuff before you pass.
- **Casting hands your opponent the initiative.** The showdown ends only when
  all players pass *in sequence* (553.4.a), so every spell resets that and buys
  them another window. Default to passing first and making them commit. The one
  exception is Lilting Lullaby — "can't play spells this turn" genuinely takes
  the last word.
- **Standard moves are illegal during a showdown** (140.1.c), but Resonating
  Strike and Smoke and Mirrors move units without exhausting them, so they can
  reinforce an exhausted body into a combat.
- **Multiple units can move simultaneously to a shared destination** (140.3), so
  Sprites can march in alongside Fae Fawn while her trigger drops a rear-guard.

## Economy

- **Power costs are paid by recycling a rune** (157.2.b) — it goes to the bottom
  of the rune deck and your board shrinks. Judge the curve by *total* cost
  (energy + power), not energy.
- **Runes only ready in your own Awaken Phase** (515.1.a). Anything you tap on
  your turn is unavailable on the opponent's, so holding up Reactions means not
  developing. (Sona - Harmonious from VEN breaks this rule and is worth a look.)
- **Only ONE of your three battlefields is used per game**, and there are only 2
  in play total (485.4 / 486.4). *How* it is picked depends on the mode, and the
  earlier note here ("chosen at random, 644.5") was only half right:
  - **Duel (Bo1)** — random from your three (485.5). All three must be
    independently strong; no battlefield "package."
  - **Match (Bo3)** — you **choose** which to present each game (486.5), and a
    battlefield used in a game someone won is burned for the match. Here the
    three want distinct roles (go-first / go-second / neutral), not three
    interchangeable generically-good picks.
  Never build the main deck around a specific battlefield either way.
- **Sequence token plays cheap-to-expensive.** The Legend ability costs {1} less
  per friendly Temporary unit, so activate it *last*. Sprite Fountain is Gear,
  not a unit — it gives no discount.
