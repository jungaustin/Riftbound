# Piloting guide — Lillia Fae Fawn (v7)

Read `patterns.md` first; this assumes the scoring model in it.

## Mulligan

You may set aside up to 2 and redraw (117.1). Keep any hand with **Sprite
Fountain** — it's the best rate in the deck and the turn-2 conquer runs off it.
Want one cheap ready-token source plus one non-Temporary body.

Ship: two or more 5-drops, or Lilting Lullaby with no Calm rune in sight.

## Turn economy

You channel 2 runes per turn (515.3.b), +1 on the draw on turn 1 only. So runes
on board ≈ 2 × turn number. Judge affordability by **total cost** — every Power
pip is a recycled rune.

Past turn 4 the deck is not energy-constrained, it's **card**-constrained. Play
the effects that replace themselves (Stupefy, Discipline, Smoke and Mirrors)
even when the effect is marginal.

## Early — turns 1-3

**Nothing you play can act the turn you play it.** Units enter exhausted (139.4)
and a standard move costs Exhaust (140.2). Only *ready* tokens can conquer
immediately: Sprite Fountain, Sprite Call, Sprite Burst, Trevor's hold trigger,
and the Legend ability.

- **T1** — deploy a cheap body. Sprite Fountain needs 3 runes (E2 + a recycled
  Mind), so it's a turn-1 play only on the draw.
- **T2** — Sprite Fountain → ready Sprite → **move it onto a battlefield →
  conquer → 1 point.** Move your turn-1 body in alongside it: the Sprite takes
  the ground, a real unit banks it. Without that second body you lose control at
  your Beginning Phase and get no Hold.
- **T3** — Trevor Snoozebottom, or Fae Fawn. Prefer playing Fae Fawn **onto a
  battlefield you already control** rather than Accelerating her out of base —
  it's cheaper and her Sprite lands somewhere useful next turn. Hide a Sprite
  Call if you have a spare rune.

Hold Stupefy and Discipline. They're Reactions; spending them on your own turn
wastes them.

## Mid — turns 4-6

This is where sequencing decides games.

- **Cheap tokens first, Legend ability last.** Two Sprites out makes it cost 2;
  four makes it free.
- **Trevor is the engine now.** He survives your Beginning Phase, so you Hold
  for a point *and* he hands you a fresh ready Sprite at that battlefield, which
  can walk over and pressure the other one. One point plus one attacker per turn
  from a 3-drop.
- **Dusk Rose Lab converts the corpse.** Your Sprites die at the start of your
  Beginning Phase anyway — cash one in for a card first. Only safe when Trevor
  or another body is there to keep control.
- **Protector of Dreams goes to base.** Her Tank grant is global; at base she
  can't be attacked. You lose the "+1 Might per token" half of the card, which
  is the price. Deploy her to a battlefield only when it's safe and you want the
  pump.
- **Zilean must be at a battlefield** to double a token play, once per turn.
  Fire him on the Legend ability or Sprite Call (you choose the location), not
  on Sprite Fountain (locked to base).

## Late — closing

Plan the last two points **a turn early**, because of the Final Point rule.

- **Thousand-Tailed Watcher is the closer.** Total cost 8 means it's live from
  turn 4 on the play, not turn 7. Its -3 hits *all* enemy units on both
  battlefields, which turns your 3-Might Sprites lethal and enables the
  double-conquer. Afterwards it's a 7-Might non-Temporary body that Holds.
- **Tank helps on offense too.** Attacking with Sprites + Watcher forces the
  defender to chew through the Sprites first, so Watcher survives to hold the
  ground he just took.
- **Save Lilting Lullaby** for the combat that decides a point.

## Showdown priority

Reinforcements that work at instant speed: **Resonating Strike** (Reaction, pull
a body in at +2), **Sprite Call** (Action, free from Hidden), **Smoke and
Mirrors** (Action).

Combat is summed Might with lethal assigned in full (626.1.d), so:

- **Smoke Screen (-4) on their biggest attacker** beats +2 on yours.
- **Stupefy (-1)** is lethal math — drop a 4 to a 3 so your 3 damage kills it.
- **Extra 3-Might Sprites are disproportionately good** — each forces a full 3
  damage and remainders can't be spread.
- **As defender you win by surviving** — if both sides have units left, the
  attackers are recalled (627.2). One extra body flips a conquer into a recall.

**Pass first and make them commit.** Every spell you cast resets the pass
sequence and gives them another window.

## The five mistakes

1. Contesting battlefields on turns 1-2 with nothing to back it up.
2. Activating the Legend before deploying your cheaper Sprite sources.
3. Trading a real unit where a Sprite would have done the job.
4. Expecting a Sprite to Hold. It cannot. Ever.
5. Tapping out. Runes don't ready until *your* Awaken Phase.
