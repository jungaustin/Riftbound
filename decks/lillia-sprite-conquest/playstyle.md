# Playstyle — Lillia Sprite Conquest

Meta toggle OFF: nothing below claims anything about matchups or what you'll face.

## Mulligan

**Keep** any hand with a turn-1 or turn-2 Sprite source plus one way to affect a fight.

- **Sprite Fountain** is the best turn-2 card in the deck (3 runes → a ready Sprite now and a second one at your next Beginning Phase). Keep every hand with it.
- **Petal Pixie** is the only true 2-rune play. She's a fine turn-1 body but she is not a keep by herself.
- Fae Fawn does **not** need to be in hand — she starts in the Champion Zone and is available every game from turn 2 (103.2.a.1). Never mulligan looking for her.
- **Ship** hands with two or more of Zilean / Soul Shepherd / Sprite Burst and nothing under 4 runes. Eight 5+ cost copies is the deck's real risk and a slow opening hand is how you lose.
- **Ship** hands of pure interaction (Back Off + Smoke Screen + Crescent Strike, no Sprite source). You have nothing to protect.

## Turns 1–2 — get a ready body into the base

Runes: 2 on T1, 4 on T2 (one more on the draw).

- T1: Petal Pixie, or nothing.
- T2: **Sprite Fountain** (3 runes) is the priority — it makes a ready Sprite immediately and hands you a second one for free next turn. Alternatively Fae Fawn from the Champion Zone (3 runes), or Fae Fawn with Accelerate for 5 if you're on the draw and want to conquer this turn.
- **Conquer as early as turn 2.** A ready Sprite in your base can standard-move to an uncontested battlefield and take it for a point immediately (630.1). There is no reason to wait — the Sprite is going to die at your Beginning Phase whether or not you used it.

## Turns 3–4 — the engine turns

Runes: 6 on T3, 8 on T4. Every 5-cost card comes online at T3.

Priority order for the turn-3 slot:

1. **Zilean** if you have a token source to double next turn. He must go **to a battlefield**, not base, so only play him where he'll survive.
2. **LeBlanc** if the opponent is out-grinding you and you need Sprites that stick. Park her at a battlefield and stack Sprites there — they stop dying and start Holding.
3. **Soul Shepherd** if the showdowns are close. +1 to every token is the difference between 9 and 12 summed Might.
4. **Sprite Mother** for tempo: a 3-Might body plus a ready 3-Might Sprite in one card.

Activate the Legend **every turn you can afford it**. With two Sprites already out it costs {2}; with four it's free. It is the only Sprite source that costs no card.

## Turns 5+ — closing

- **Sprite Burst** is the double-conquer turn: two ready Sprites, four with Zilean out. Hold it for a turn where both battlefields are takeable — 2 points in one turn is a quarter of the game (victory is 8).
- If the board has stalled, switch axes: commit LeBlanc and stop cycling Sprites. Held battlefields score every Beginning Phase and don't require winning a fight each turn.

## Showdown priority

Combat is **summed Might vs summed Might**, and you only conquer if *every* defender dies and *one* attacker lives (627.3). Spend resources in this order:

1. **Charm** (E1 P1) — *before* the fight, not during. Move the defender off the battlefield entirely and the showdown never happens; you walk in and Conquer. It works on any Might, so it's the answer to a blocker your swarm simply cannot out-Might. Needs a **Calm** rune (74.5% by T2, 90.9% by T3), so plan around whether you've channelled one.
2. **Back Off** (stun the biggest defender) — a stunned unit deals **zero** combat damage, so all your Sprites survive the fight. The cheapest way to turn a losing attack into a clean conquest once combat is already happening.
3. **Smoke Screen** (−4) — either kills a ≤4-Might blocker outright or drops their summed Might below what your swarm can absorb.
4. **Discipline** (+2, draw 1) — for margins of 1–2. It replaces itself, so it's nearly free.
5. **Sprite Call from Hidden** — flip it for **{0 energy}** *after* they've committed, for a surprise 3-Might attacker.

## Your Beginning Phase is a decision point, not a formality

This is the part v1/v2 got wrong. The Temporary trigger goes **on the chain** and can be **responded to** — do not let it resolve on autopilot:

- [ ] Is a Temporary token about to die, and is it my only unit at that battlefield?
- [ ] Do I have a card Hidden at **that** battlefield? Flipping it is Reaction speed and {0 energy}.
- [ ] **Respond to the trigger — do not let it resolve first.** While it's pending the old token is still alive, so you still control the battlefield and the Hidden card is still valid. Flip it, the new Sprite enters, *then* the trigger resolves and kills only the old one. The new token is present at the Scoring Step and **holds for a point**.
- [ ] Only **Reaction** speed can answer the trigger. Sprite Call **from hand is Action speed and cannot** — this line only exists if you pre-hid the card on an earlier turn. Ambush units and Reaction spells also qualify; energy is irrelevant, it's a speed wall.
- [ ] If Dusk Rose Lab is the battlefield: flip the Hidden replacement **first**, then feed the old token to the Lab for a card, then let Temporary fizzle. +1 card, battlefield kept, point scored.

Practical consequence: **hide a Sprite Call every turn you can spare the rune.** It is the difference between a battlefield that scores once and one that scores every turn.

**Note the hole:** v2 has no hard removal. Charm and Back Off delay; they don't kill. Against one large Deflect or Tank unit you will be relocating or stunning it every turn rather than answering it, and you win by re-conquering faster than they can re-establish.

## Sequencing rules that matter

- **Move Fae Fawn last.** She leaves a Sprite wherever she moves *from*, so the last thing you do each turn should be relocating her — you get a free garrison and she ends up where you need her.
- **Use spent Sprites before your Beginning Phase.** Anything a Sprite could still do (exhausting for a cost, absorbing damage) is free value — they die regardless.
- **Don't over-commit to a battlefield you already control.** You can't Conquer something you control (630.1); a Sprite parked on your own battlefield scores nothing and then dies. Send it at the *other* battlefield.
- **Zilean and LeBlanc have to be exposed to work.** Both require being at a battlefield. Assume they will be attacked, and keep Sprites with them as damage sponges.
