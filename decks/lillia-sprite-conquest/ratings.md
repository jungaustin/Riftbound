Legend: **Lillia - Bashful Bloom** — `{4 energy}, Exhaust: Play a ready 3 Might Sprite unit token with [Temporary]. This ability costs {1 energy} less for each friendly unit with [Temporary].`
Chosen Champion: **Lillia - Fae Fawn**
Identity: Calm+Mind · Meta toggle: **OFF** (no matchup or tier claims below)

## The frame everything is rated against

Three rules facts drive every score here, verified against the Core Rules:

1. ~~**Temporary units can never Hold.**~~ **WRONG — see the correction in [patterns.md](patterns.md) and the README.** Every rating below was made under this false premise, so the Hidden/holding cards are systematically under-rated here. Temporary is a chain-using trigger you can **respond** to; a Reaction-speed replacement leaves a token alive at the Scoring Step that holds and scores (`reference/riftbound_temporary_interactions.txt`). What remains true: **Conquer scores immediately** (630.1) on gaining control of a battlefield you haven't scored this turn, which is still the deck's fastest income. Cards whose scores move most on the correction: **Sprite Call** (84 → higher, it's a per-turn holding engine, not just an ambush), **Dusk Rose Lab** (56 → top-tier for this deck), **LeBlanc** (88, still strong but no longer the only route to holding), and every Ambush/Reaction body rated in the 55-65 band.
2. **Units enter exhausted** (139.4). A Sprite printed "ready" can standard-move to a battlefield the turn it appears — that is the entire difference between a Sprite that scores and a Sprite that sits there.
3. **You can only play a unit to a location you control** (559.2). You can never drop a unit onto an enemy battlefield. Every point comes from *moving* a ready body in. Ready base Sprites are conquest fuel; Sprites made at a battlefield you already control are garrison.

Consequence: after a Sprite conquers and then dies at your Beginning Phase, the battlefield becomes uncontrolled again — **so you can conquer the same battlefield again next turn**. The deck scores 1–2 per turn and never has to defend what it took. 1v1 uses 2 battlefields, victory at 8 (644.3–644.4).

Rating bands: 85+ core · 70–84 strong · 55–69 conditional · 35–54 fringe · <35 unplayable here.

## Combo clusters, named

**1. The conquest loop (3 pieces, all cheap).** Legend + any ready-Sprite source + a stun.
Sprite Fountain / Sprite Call / Sprite Burst / the Legend put a **ready** Sprite in your base. It standard-moves to a battlefield you don't control (139.4 + 140.4.a), you win the showdown, and you **Conquer for an immediate point** (630.1). At your next Beginning Phase the Sprite dies (728.1.b), the battlefield goes uncontrolled — **so you can conquer it again next turn**. Back Off / Smoke Screen make the showdown math work. You never defend what you score.

**2. The Legend discount spiral (self-reinforcing, 2+ pieces).** The Legend costs {4} minus one energy per friendly Temporary unit. Every Sprite already on board makes the *next* Sprite cheaper: 2 Sprites out → the Legend activates for {2}; 4 out → **free**. Sprite Fountain's Deathknell Sprite is the cheapest way to prime this on turn 2.

**3. Zilean doubling (2 pieces, multiplicative).** Zilean at a battlefield doubles one token play per turn. Sprite Burst becomes **four** ready Sprites. Sprite Mother becomes a body plus two Sprites. The Legend's free activation becomes two Sprites. He has to be committed to a battlefield, not sitting in base.

**4. LeBlanc permanence (2 pieces, plan-changing).** LeBlanc at a battlefield switches off Temporary there. Every Sprite you park at her battlefield survives the Beginning Phase and **Holds for a point** (630.2), and Backline keeps her alive behind them. This converts the deck from "1 conquer point per turn" to "conquer *and* hold" and is why she is a 3-of.

**5. Might conversion (2 pieces).** Soul Shepherd makes every Sprite a 4; Petal Pixie grows +1 per Temporary unit beside her. Three Sprites + Shepherd = 12 summed Might instead of 9; three Sprites + Pixie = a 5-Might Pixie for E2. Combat is summed Might vs summed Might with lethal-damage assignment (626.1.b–d), so raw totals are what decide conquest.

**6. Fae Fawn relocation (2 pieces).** Smoke and Mirrors (or Forgotten Signpost / Resonating Strike / Emperor's Divide) moves Fae Fawn out of a location; she leaves a Sprite behind holding it for the turn, and Smoke and Mirrors also draws. One card, two board positions.

## Traps (cards that look right here and aren't)

- **Spectral Centaur** — "+2 when another friendly unit dies" expires at end of turn, and your Sprites die in the *Beginning* Phase, before you could ever use it.
- **Mushroom Pouch** — wants a facedown card at a battlefield, but Hidden cards are trashed when you have no unit there (523), and your units there are Temporary and die every turn.
- **Otterpus** — denies *early* points; this deck's whole plan is scoring on turns 2–3, so it taxes me harder than the opponent.
- **Unchecked Power** — 12 damage to ALL units at battlefields wipes a swarm far harder than it wipes a single fat defender.
- **Promising Future / Party Favors / Card Sharp / The Arena's Greatest** — symmetric effects that hand the opponent the same resources while I'm the one already ahead on board.
- **Forgotten Monument** (battlefield) — no scoring until turn 3, which is exactly the window this deck is built to exploit.
- **Bottled Constellation** — the sacrifice happens at the start of your *Main* Phase; your Sprites already died in the Beginning Phase, so you'd be sacrificing fresh bodies you wanted attacking.
